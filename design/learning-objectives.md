# Learning objectives

Measurable, observable objectives. Each one maps to practice, feedback and assessment in [alignment-matrix.md](alignment-matrix.md).

By the end of the course, the learner will be able to:

1. **Identify hazards from visual cues and rate their risk.** Given a realistic warehouse area, flag the conditions that are hazards and rate each one by likelihood (1–5) and severity (1–5), landing in the same risk band (low / medium / high) as the reference rating.
2. **Tell real hazards from controlled conditions.** Given conditions that look risky (a ramped cable, a wrapped tall stack, a guarded machine, a striped gate), correctly leave them unflagged or explain why they are controlled.
3. **Analyze an incident to its contributing causes.** Given a near-miss sequence, identify causes at three levels (condition, behavior, system) and choose the right action at each decision point.
4. **Select controls using the hierarchy of controls.** For a given hazard, rank candidate controls so higher-level controls (elimination, engineering) come before administrative controls and PPE.
5. **Report a hazard clearly to a supervisor.** In a conversation with a busy supervisor, report using the situation, hazard, impact, ask structure, with one specific request.

Language level: plain English at about CEFR B1 throughout, because the audience includes temporary staff and non-native speakers.
