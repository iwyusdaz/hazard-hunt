# Personas

All personas are fictional, constructed from the plan's audience description for design purposes. They are not based on research participants.

| Persona | Situation | Need | How the design responds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dario, 22, new associate | Knows the rules from slides; doesn't notice hazards in a busy space | Practice noticing, with feedback | Practice zone with hints, then an assessed zone without them |
| Grace, 41, team lead | Coaches others and escalates hazards | Confidence to escalate; a model for good reports | The replay's causes chart; the speak-up scenario (she also appears as the supervisor in it) |
| Ahmed, 30, temporary worker | Intermediate English | Visual, low-text interaction | B1 plain language, icons with labels, 3D selection, text size settings |
| Ms. Patel, safety manager | Wants to know which hazards learners miss | Data she can use | Local learning record per hazard (download as JSON); the manager guide explains how this could be aggregated in a real deployment, with privacy cautions |
