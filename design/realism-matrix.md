# Realism matrix

Rule: high realism only where noticing the detail is the skill being learned; simplify anything that competes for attention.

| Scene element | Realism | Learning cue? | Rationale | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hazard cues: spill sheen, frayed cable, bent upright, overhang, missing guard, leaning stack, red dock signal | High | Yes | Recognizing these is objective 1 | Realistic detail helps when task-relevant ([PMC11269326](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11269326/)) |
| Distractors: ramped cable, wrapped stack, guarded machine, striped gate | High | Yes | Discrimination (objective 2) only works if the distractor looks as convincing as the hazard | Same |
| Floor (CC0 worn concrete, matte) | Medium–high | Partly | Gives scale and makes the glossy spill readable by contrast | Same |
| Racking, pallets, boxes | Medium (PBR textures on uprights and pallets, flat boxes) | Partly | Realistic clutter is part of the difficulty; boxes kept plain so the overhang and lean read clearly | Same |
| Image-based lighting (CC0 Empty Warehouse HDRI) | Medium | No | Plausible reflections and light falloff; not shown as a background | — |
| Workers | Low: faceless hi-vis silhouettes at a distance | Partly | Pedestrian position matters in the replay; faces do not. Avoids uncanny figures | Plan 04 §6.1 |
| Reach trucks | Medium | Yes (replay) | Movement path and blind spots matter | Plan 04 §6.1 |
| Walls, roof, dock doors | Low | No | Context only; kept plain and dim | Seductive details harm learning ([2025 meta-analysis](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s10648-025-10099-z.pdf)) |
| Sparks, smoke, alarms, injury | None | No | No dramatization; near-miss only | Plan 04 §4 safety rules |

**Signaling:** practice-zone hints are text plus an icon, never color alone. The assessed zone has no hints and lists every inspectable area in the points-of-interest list.
