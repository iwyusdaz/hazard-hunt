**Total:** 14 + 18 + 16 + 14 + 15 = **77 s**, inside the 90 s budget. The replay is learner-paced (play, pause, scrub, orbit a frozen moment), so the learner sets the pace; it counts toward the budget anyway.

**Rules applied:** every shot is skippable from the first frame (Skip button, Esc), captioned, and has an audio-description text in the data. None plays automatically and none plays during an assessment item. A content note appears before the replay.
