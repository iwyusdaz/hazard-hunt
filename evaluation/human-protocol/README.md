# Human testing protocol (templates, not yet used)

No sessions have been run. These templates exist so real playtests can be added later.

## Consent (read aloud or give in writing)
> You're invited to try a short safety training activity and tell us what was clear or confusing. It takes about 45 minutes. We are testing the course, not you. You can stop at any time without giving a reason. We won't record your name with your answers. Screen activity may be recorded only if you agree. Do you agree to take part? Do you agree to screen recording?

Do not include anyone under 18. Collect no personal data beyond what the session needs.

## Session script
1. Consent (above). Note device and browser.
2. "Please think aloud as you go." Start at Module 1.
3. Observe without helping. Note where the participant hesitates, misreads, or asks a question.
4. After Module 2 Part B: "Which items did you decide not to report, and why?"
5. After the replay: "What caused the near-miss?"
6. Post-session: 3 questions — easiest part, hardest part, anything that felt unrealistic.
