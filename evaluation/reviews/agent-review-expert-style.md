# Agent review: expert-style persona reviews

Agent review — simulated persona. These are a reviewer agent's simulations of how two named personas from the plan (§10) might respond. They are not reviews by a real workplace safety trainer or L&D lead. Domain points were checked against the repo's own `research/sources.md` and general knowledge, and should be confirmed by a qualified person.
